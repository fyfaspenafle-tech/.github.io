Business questions and methodology

Which warehouse has the highest on-time delivery rate? How do stockout incidents and shipping costs vary? Filter the data to explore segments and identify candidates for further investigation.

Definitions: On-time rate = shipments marked on time / all shipments. Stockout rate = shipments with a stockout incident / all shipments. Average shipping cost = total shipping costs / shipment count. Missing periods are omitted from the line chart.

Limitations: Synthetic data cannot establish real-world causes or justify operational investment. Differences need validation with genuine data, service-level agreements and staffing context.
