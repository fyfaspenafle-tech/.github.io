E-commerce Sales Analysis

SQL analysis of a simulated UK retail order dataset from 2025

SQLData cleaningData visualisation

Business question

Which months, product categories and UK regions contributed most to completed-order revenue in 2025?

Data transparency: This is a practice project using 1,200 synthetic orders, not real company data. Findings are reproducible from the included CSV and SQL.

Method

Imported order records, filtered to order_status = 'Completed', and grouped sales by month, category and region. Used SQL SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, SUM, COUNT, AVG and ORDER BY.

Returned and cancelled orders are excluded from completed-order revenue. The dataset does not model net profit or refunds.

Interpretation and limitations

Home Office generated the most completed-order revenue in this simulation. This is descriptive analysis, not proof of demand or a recommendation to increase inventory. A real decision would also require margins, return costs and multi-year seasonality.
